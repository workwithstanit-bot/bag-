Scrape. Catch. Refresh.

1

Scrape

Run your studs across the rubber fins on the front. The nylon brush strip gets the mud packed between them.

2

Catch

Drop the cleats in and zip it. A center divider keeps them apart, and whatever mud is left falls into the tray.

3

At home, slide the tray out, dump it and rinse it. Mesh vents and bamboo charcoal pods take care of the smell.
